gender.onnx: voice gender classifier (ECAPA-TDNN), MIT License.

Weights: JaesungHuh/voice-gender-classifier (https://huggingface.co/JaesungHuh/voice-gender-classifier), MIT.
ONNX export with int8 quantization: Alice-Sabrina-Ivy/voice-gender-classifier-onnx-q8-v2
(https://huggingface.co/Alice-Sabrina-Ivy/voice-gender-classifier-onnx-q8-v2), MIT.
Input: mono float32 audio at 16 kHz, shape (batch, time). Output: logits over {0: male, 1: female}.
